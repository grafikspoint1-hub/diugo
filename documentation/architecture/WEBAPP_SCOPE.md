# MY DIU Web Application — Portal & Module Map

## Portals

### Public / Guest
Homepage, global search, category pages, business directory, storefronts, products, events, destinations, transport/service discovery, emergency information, deals, support and vendor application.

### Customer
Profile, addresses, wishlist, cart, checkout, orders, booking records, ticket records, reviews, notifications, support tickets, rewards/membership.

### Vendor
Application and document submission, verification status, business profile, storefront, products, inventory, orders, bookings (where enabled), offers/coupons, reviews, notifications, analytics, payout/settlement view and support.

### Delivery Partner
Application and verification, online/offline availability, assigned jobs, pickup/drop details, delivery status, proof of delivery, history, earnings and support.

### Super Admin
Dashboard, customer and role management, vendor verification, categories, products, orders, booking providers, booking policies, delivery dispatch, events and organizers, tourism content, transport providers, emergency contacts, offers, reviews, support, CMS/banners, SEO, payments/refunds, settlements, reports, settings and audit logs.

## Operating rules

- Public pages show approved and active records only.
- Vendors can access only their own business, products, bookings and orders.
- Customers can access only their own accounts and transactions.
- Admin access is permission-controlled and audited.
- Emergency contact details require a source and verification date before publication.
- Payment confirmation must come from a verified server-side gateway webhook.
- Prices and money amounts are stored in integer paise in the backend.
- The website must remain responsive across phone, tablet and desktop browsers.
- English, Gujarati and Hindi are required language options; translated content must be reviewed before publication.
